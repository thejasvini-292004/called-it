Don't just watch the game. Lead it.

Every fan gets the same match situation and the same deadline. You make the call, the real captain makes theirs, the over plays out, and an open-source AI model explains both sides using only the match data on the right. Your Match IQ scores the decision, never the luck.

Match feed

Events as the server records them. The server clock sets the deadline for every fan.

Fair-play rules

  • Everyone sees the same situation at the same moment.
  • One deadline for all: the window shuts when the bowler starts his run-up.
  • You can change your pick until the deadline. Whatever is selected then is locked.
  • Picks made after the deadline are saved for learning but never scored.
  • Match IQ rewards the quality of the call. The result of one over is not scored.
